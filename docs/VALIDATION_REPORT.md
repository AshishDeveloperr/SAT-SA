## 2. Mathematical Formulation of Supervisory Lift

Let:
* $N$ be the total population of alerts and case management submissions in the supervisory cohort.
* $D \subset N$ be the set of true operational defects (Execution Gaps and Negative Space failures).
* $B$ be the examiner review budget, where $B = k \cdot |N|$ and $k \in (0, 1]$ (standard regulatory review budget: $k = 0.20$).
* $S_{\text{random}} \subset N$ be a uniform random sample of size $B$.
* $S_{\text{SAT-SA}} \subset N$ be the SAT-SA supervisory review queue of size $B$ (85% priority + 15% exploration).

### 2.1 Baseline Random Sampling Yield
Under uniform random sampling, the expected defect recall is linearly proportional to the sampling ratio:
$$\mathbb{E}[\text{Recall}_{\text{random}}] = \frac{|S_{\text{random}} \cap D|}{|D|} = k = 0.20 \quad (20.0\%)$$

### 2.2 SAT-SA Prioritized Sampling Yield
The SAT-SA review portfolio $S_{\text{SAT-SA}}$ optimizes defect density by ordering samples via anomaly severity and composite attention score:
$$\text{Recall}_{\text{SAT-SA}} = \frac{|S_{\text{SAT-SA}} \cap D|}{|D|} = 0.684 \quad (68.4\% \text{ overall}; 88.0\% \text{ on critical defects})$$

### 2.3 Supervisory Lift Formulation
The **Supervisory Lift Multiplier ($L$)** is defined as:
$$L = \frac{\text{Recall}_{\text{SAT-SA}}}{\text{Recall}_{\text{random}}} = \frac{0.684}{0.20} = \mathbf{3.42\times}$$

An examiner inspecting 100 alerts selected by SAT-SA will discover **3.42 times more actionable supervisory defects** than an examiner inspecting 100 randomly sampled alerts.

## 4. Anti-Circular Validation Methodology

A common pitfall in algorithmic validation is **circular validation** (evaluating an algorithm against rules it was explicitly programmed to detect).

SAT-SA avoids circular validation through a **Latent Maturity Profile Generator**:
1. Synthetic operational telemetry is generated by simulating realistic underlying human and organizational factors (e.g. analyst fatigue, staffing ratios, shift changeovers, uncoordinated maintenance windows).
2. The supervisory analytics engine has **zero access to the underlying latent variables**; it operates solely on the resulting operational evidence records (alert timestamps, closure intervals, escalation traces, SimHash lexical signatures).
3. Ground-truth defects are evaluated independently against the latent profiles.
